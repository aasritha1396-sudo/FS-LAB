About the Training & Placement Department

The Training and Placement Department of Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences (ANITS) plays a vital role in preparing students for successful careers. The department organizes placement drives, technical training, soft skills sessions, mock interviews, and career guidance programs to enhance students' employability.

Vision

To empower students with the skills, knowledge, and confidence required to achieve successful careers in leading national and international organizations.

Mission

To provide quality training, establish strong industry connections, and create excellent placement opportunities for every eligible student.

Objectives

  • Improve students' technical skills.
  • Conduct aptitude and reasoning training.
  • Organize mock interviews and group discussions.
  • Invite reputed companies for campus recruitment.
  • Guide students in career planning.
